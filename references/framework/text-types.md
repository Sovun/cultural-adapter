# Text Types

The same culture profile applies differently depending on what kind of text is being reviewed. This file tells a reviewer which dimensions and profile sections matter most for each text type, and which mistakes are most costly.

"Sensitivity" means how much a 1-point gap on that dimension should matter for this text type (see [dimensions.md](dimensions.md)).

## Structure by format

Default skeletons. The culture profile decides how long each part is and in which order the context and the ask come.

- **Formal email:** salutation with title, courtesy line, purpose, details, explicit next step with date, formal sign-off, full signature.
- **Informal email:** greeting by name, purpose in the first lines, short details, next step, light sign-off.
- **Chat message:** greeting only if the culture expects one, one idea per message, the ask stated plainly, no long blocks.
- **Presentation:** each slide title states its message; a context or relationship slide before the problem where the culture is relationship-first; one idea per slide; summary and a next-step slide phrased to suit the reader's seniority.
- **Report or long document:** executive summary first, then context, findings, recommendations, appendix; headings that can be skimmed.
- **Proposal:** who we are and why, understanding of the client's situation, solution, value and evidence, plan and cost, next step.

## Business email and correspondence

**Most relevant dimensions:** D1 Directness, D3 Formality, D4 Hierarchy, D5 Relationship, D9 Time precision, D10 Argument style.
**Sensitivity:** high for salutation, sign-off and requests to senior readers.

Focus on:
- Salutation, honorifics, name order and sign-off ("Addressing people").
- Where the ask appears (first line vs after context).
- How refusals, delays and criticism are phrased.
- Explicitness of deadlines, owners and next steps.
- Length and amount of courtesy text.

## Marketing, advertising and landing pages

**Most relevant dimensions:** D6 Expressiveness, D7 Self-promotion, D8 Group orientation.
**Sensitivity:** very high for legal and symbolic issues; high for tone.

Focus on:
- Claims and superlatives against local advertising law ("Persuasion, marketing and self-presentation").
- Individual vs family/community benefit framing.
- Imagery, colors, numbers, gestures and religious or national symbols.
- Holidays and timing.
- Language variant and mandatory local-language rules.
- Humor and wordplay.

## Product UI, onboarding and microcopy

**Most relevant dimensions:** D3 Formality, D6 Expressiveness, D2 Context reliance.
**Sensitivity:** high for formality consistency and formats.

Focus on:
- T/V choice (tu/vous, du/Sie, ты/Ви) applied consistently across the product.
- Formats: dates, numbers, currency, names, addresses, phone numbers, RTL.
- Gendered grammar (Hebrew, Arabic, Polish, Ukrainian, French).
- Error messages: blame-free and appropriately polite.
- Text expansion (German, Finnish and Russian are often 30% longer than English) and truncation.
- Name and address forms that do not assume Western structure.

## CVs, cover letters and professional bios

**Most relevant dimensions:** D7 Self-promotion, D8 Group orientation, D3 Formality.
**Sensitivity:** very high for self-promotion and legally allowed personal data.

Focus on:
- How strongly achievements are claimed ("I led" with metrics vs modest "contributed to").
- Personal data norms: photo, date of birth, marital status, nationality, religion (expected in some markets, discouraged or illegal to request in others).
- Length and format (one-page résumé in the US, tabular Lebenslauf in Germany, rirekisho in Japan, RODO clause in Poland).
- Education and title prominence.

## Job advertisements and HR communication

**Most relevant dimensions:** D4 Hierarchy, D7 Self-promotion, D8 Group orientation.
**Sensitivity:** very high for anti-discrimination law.

Focus on:
- Discriminatory or coded wording (age, gender, nationality, military service).
- Required gender markers (e.g. "(m/w/d)" in Germany, "(K/M)" in Poland).
- Data that may legally be requested.
- Tone of employer branding (individual growth vs team belonging, stability vs dynamism).

## Feedback, performance reviews and difficult messages

**Most relevant dimensions:** D1 Directness, D4 Hierarchy, D2 Context reliance.
**Sensitivity:** very high.

Focus on:
- How criticism is framed and where it is delivered (private vs group).
- Softeners or their absence.
- Upgraders and downgraders ("totally unacceptable" vs "a bit concerning").
- Face-saving language and alternatives offered.

## Negotiation, proposals and sales outreach

**Most relevant dimensions:** D5 Relationship, D10 Argument style, D7 Self-promotion, D9 Time precision.
**Sensitivity:** high.

Focus on:
- Credentials and company introduction (expected in China and India, less so in the Netherlands).
- Pressure tactics, expiring offers and ultimatums (counterproductive in Türkiye, Japan, Mexico).
- Evidence types: data and specifications vs relationships and references.
- Price discussion timing.

## Social media and community content

**Most relevant dimensions:** D6 Expressiveness, D8 Group orientation.
**Sensitivity:** very high for legal speech risks and trending slang.

Focus on:
- Slang, memes and emoji that may age quickly or misfire.
- Political, religious and historical references.
- Local platform norms (WeChat, LINE, KakaoTalk, WhatsApp).
- Speech laws that apply to posts (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, China).

## Technical documentation

**Most relevant dimensions:** D2 Context reliance, D9 Time precision.
**Sensitivity:** low for tone, high for clarity and formats.

Focus on:
- Plain, consistent terminology for non-native readers.
- Units, formats, and locale-specific examples.
- Idioms and humor removed.

## Public statements, apologies and crisis communication

**Most relevant dimensions:** D1 Directness, D4 Hierarchy, D8 Group orientation.
**Sensitivity:** very high.

Focus on:
- Who apologizes (seniority of the signatory matters in Japan, Korea, China).
- Degree of responsibility taken and how it is phrased.
- Sensitive historical, political and religious context.
- Channel and timing.
