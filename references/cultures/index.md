## Reading the matrix

- **Biggest directness gaps** (D1): Netherlands, Germany and Israel (5) versus Japan and Indonesia (1). Texts moving between these groups need the most rework.
- **Formality** (D3): Japan and South Korea (5) versus Israel (1) and the US (2).
- **Self-promotion** (D7): the US (5) versus the UK, Netherlands, Japan and Finland (1). This matters most for CVs, bios and marketing.
- **Argument order** (D10): conclusion-first cultures (US, Netherlands, Israel, Finland) versus context-first ones (France, Germany, Japan, China, Ukraine).
