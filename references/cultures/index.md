## Reading the matrix

- **Directness** (D1): Israel and the Netherlands (5) versus Japan, Indonesia and Thailand (1). Texts moving between these groups need the most rework. Germany, Finland, Denmark, Norway, Austria and Poland sit at 4; most of Asia, the Gulf and Latin America at 2.
- **Context** (D2): Germany, the Netherlands and the US (1) versus Japan (5). Most of Asia, the Middle East, Africa and Latin America sit at 4.
- **Formality** (D3): Japan and South Korea (5) versus Israel (1) and the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland and the Nordics (2).
- **Hierarchy** (D4): Denmark, Norway, Sweden, the Netherlands and Israel (1) versus East and Southeast Asia, the Gulf, Egypt, Pakistan and Nigeria (5).
- **Relationship first** (D5): the US, Canada, Australia, Germany, Switzerland, the Netherlands and the Nordics (2) versus Egypt and Saudi Arabia (5).
- **Self-promotion** (D7): the US (5) versus Sweden (1). This matters most for CVs, bios and marketing.
- **Time precision** (D9): Switzerland, Germany, Finland and Japan (5) versus much of Latin America, the Middle East and South and Southeast Asia (2).
- **Argument order** (D10): the US (1) and, at 2, the UK, Nordics, Netherlands, Canada, Australia, Singapore and Israel put the conclusion first. France, Germany, Austria, Italy, China, Ukraine and much of Asia and the Middle East (4) build context first. Routine email is purpose-first almost everywhere; the score matters most for proposals, refusals and sensitive requests.
- **Neighbours are not interchangeable:** Spain vs Mexico, Portugal vs Brazil, Austria and Switzerland vs Germany, Taiwan vs mainland China, Qatar vs Saudi Arabia and the UAE all differ in scores and, more often, in vocabulary, formats and legal points. Read the profile, not just the row.
